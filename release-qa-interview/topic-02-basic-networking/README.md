# Release QA Interview Prep — Networking

Basic networking for a new-grad, junior-to-mid release QA interview.

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `networking-basics.md` | Five-part knowledge document: fundamentals, topologies, addressing, protocols, troubleshooting |
| `qa-100.md` | 100 open questions with hidden answers |
| `mcq-150.md` | 150 multiple-choice questions with hidden answers and explanations |
| `larger-prefixes-practice.md` | 10 Q&A and 10 MCQs focused on /8, /16, and /24 |

## How to use

1. Read the matching section of `networking-basics.md` first.
2. Answer a question aloud or in writing, then click **Show answer**.
3. For an MCQ, choose A–D before revealing, and read the explanation even when you are right.

## Coverage

| Part | Q&A | MCQs | Topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Fundamentals and models | 15 | 20 | network types, client-server vs peer-to-peer, OSI vs TCP/IP, encapsulation, bandwidth/latency/throughput |
| 2. Topologies and devices | 15 | 25 | bus, star, ring, mesh, tree, hybrid; hub, switch, router, access point, firewall, load balancer, proxy |
| 3. Addressing and subnetting | 25 | 40 | IPv4, public and private ranges, CIDR, masks, /24–/28 subnetting, gateway, IPv6 basics |
| 4. Protocols and ports | 25 | 35 | TCP/UDP, handshake, ports, HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, DHCP, ARP, ICMP, TLS, SSH, email, NTP, SNMP |
| 5. Troubleshooting and commands | 20 | 30 | diagnostic ladder, Windows/macOS/Linux commands, output reading, bug reports |

For the larger-prefix material in Part 3, `larger-prefixes-practice.md` adds
focused drills on /8, /16, and /24.

Later topics will sit beside this folder as `topic-03-…` and so on.
